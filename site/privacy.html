Privacy Notice

How Pro-Lec uses the personal information you send through this website.

Pro-Lec Electrical & Construction respects your privacy and processes personal information in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA).

What we collect

When you use our enquiry form, we collect your name, contact number, email address, the type of enquiry and your message. If you contact us by WhatsApp, Facebook, phone or email, we receive the details you share there.

Why we collect it

We use your information only to respond to your enquiry, prepare quotations and communicate with you about your project. We do not sell your information or use it for unrelated marketing.

Who we share it with

Enquiry form messages are delivered to us by a third-party form service. Apart from that, we only share your information where the law requires it or where you have agreed.

Maps and third-party content

Our Contact page shows an embedded Google Maps map. When that page loads, Google may collect information such as your IP address and set cookies, under Google's privacy policy. We do not receive this information.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details only for as long as needed to deal with your enquiry or project, or as required by law.

Your rights

You may ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or object to how we use it. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator (South Africa).
